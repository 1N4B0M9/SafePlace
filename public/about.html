About Harmony Watch

"Creating safer communities by tracking and visualizing incidents of hate and discrimination"

Our Mission

Harmony Watch is dedicated to creating awareness about hate crimes and discrimination in our communities. By providing a platform for reporting and visualizing these incidents, we aim to foster understanding, promote safety, and work towards building more inclusive communities.

How It Works

Our platform allows community members to report incidents of hate crimes and discrimination. These reports are then visualized on an interactive map, helping to identify patterns and areas of concern. The data is categorized by type of incident, including race, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, and disability-based discrimination.

Features

• Interactive map visualization with heatmap overlay
• Categorized incident reporting
• Community directory of reported incidents
• Dark theme design for better accessibility
• Mobile-responsive interface

Privacy & Safety

We take privacy and safety seriously. All reports are handled with care, and personal information is protected. The platform focuses on incident reporting and community awareness while maintaining the privacy of individuals involved.

Map Data & Technology

This application uses the following open-source technologies and data sources:

• Leaflet - Open-source JavaScript library for mobile-friendly interactive maps

• OpenStreetMap - Free, editable map data created by volunteers worldwide

• CARTO - Dark Matter map tiles for enhanced visualization

• Leaflet.heat - Heatmap visualization plugin
